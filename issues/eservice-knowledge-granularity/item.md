# Review the granularity of e-service knowledge once the skill is in use

## Description

ADR-10 accepted 2026-11-21 with one condition from the decider: once the
`e-services` skill is in use, watch whether what agents record and
contribute has the right granularity. Too coarse (whole-page walkthroughs,
session narratives) makes entries hard to verify and quick to go stale; too
fine (one-off details of one session) floods the references with noise.

## Wanted

After the first real contributions have arrived (and after a month of use by
the production companies, whichever comes first): review the local
`eservice-` company facts and the submitted observations, judge their
granularity against ADR-10's entry format, and propose any change to the
format or to the skill's guidance on what to record. Report the findings;
change nothing without the decider.
