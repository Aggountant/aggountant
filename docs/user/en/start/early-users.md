# Join as an early user

aggountant starts from one idea: in the age of AI agents, nobody should have to
pay for a basic service like bookkeeping. The agent can do it, and you approve.
The goal is that a small Finnish company keeps its books and makes every
statutory filing from start to finish without an accounting firm, in a few
quarter-hours of work a month.

This page is for you if you want to start using aggountant now, while it is
still early, or to build it with us.

## What we offer

- **We meet.** If you are considering aggountant for your company, we can set
  up a meeting and go through your company's situation together.
- **We build what you need.** Features come from what users' bookkeeping runs
  into. When your company meets a situation aggountant cannot handle yet, it
  goes into the work queue.
- **We answer quickly.** Questions and bug reports are usually handled within
  days, not weeks.

## What we hope for

- **Tell us about your company:** line of business, size, bank, and how the
  books are kept today. It helps decide what to build next.
- **Tell us what does not work.** Feedback from real use is the most valuable
  thing an early user gives.
- **Build with us.** aggountant is open source (MIT). We hope others,
  developers, bookkeepers and users alike, will join in improving it in the
  open-source spirit: code, documentation, fixes or experience.

## Get in touch

- Write in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/aggountant/aggountant/discussions)
  or open an [issue](https://github.com/aggountant/aggountant/issues).
- Or send email: jari.mustonen@iki.fi.

Do not post real bookkeeping material in a public discussion. Describe the
situation in general terms; the details can wait for a meeting.
