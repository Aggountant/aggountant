# Join as an early user

You can start using aggountant now, while it is still early, or help build it
with us.

aggountant started from the idea that in the age of AI agents, nobody should
have to pay for a basic service like bookkeeping. The agent can do it, and you
approve. The goal is that a small Finnish company keeps its books and makes
every statutory filing from start to finish without an accounting firm, in a
few quarter-hours of work a month.

## What we offer

Features come from what users' bookkeeping runs into. When your company meets
a situation aggountant cannot handle yet, it goes into the work queue.

## What we hope for

- Tell us your company's line of business, size and bank, and how the books
  are kept today. That is what we use to decide what to build next.
- Tell us what does not work. Feedback from real use is the most valuable
  thing an early user can give.
- Build with us, if you like. aggountant is open source (MIT), and we hope
  other developers, bookkeepers and users will join in. Code, documentation,
  fixes and experience are all welcome.

## Get in touch

Write in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/aggountant/aggountant/discussions)
or open an [issue](https://github.com/aggountant/aggountant/issues). You can
also send email to jari.mustonen@iki.fi.

Discussions and issues are public, so do not post real bookkeeping material
there. Describe the situation in general terms. You can send the
details by email.
